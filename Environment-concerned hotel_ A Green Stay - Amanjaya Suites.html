ECO-FRIENDLY ACTIONS

By Amanjaya Pancam Hotel

As of September 2023

Amanjaya Pancam Hotel in Phnom Penh, is excited to share with you all the actions we are undertaking to save the planet. We are committed to reduce our carbon footprint in the environment and have taken a number of steps to do so.We have started with small changes such as using LED bulbs instead of traditional lightbulbs, installing solar-powered lighta on our rooftop, and replacing all our appliances with energy efficient models. This has drastically reduced our electricity bills and saved us money in the long term. We have also switched to renewable energy sources such as solar, hydro and wind power for our electricity needs.Moreover, we are actively involved in minimizing the use of single-use plastics by providing reusable bottles and containers to all guests. To reduce our water consumption we have installed low-flow showerheads, toilet flushers and taps in all rooms.

Lights

Firstly, we have made significant changes to our lighting infrastructure during our renovation back in 2020, by switching to energy-efficient LED lights. We were able to save about 15% on our monthly energy consumption.

We have also installed some amazing solar garden lamps, which not only reduced our consumption but it also added a touch of elegance to our bar, Le Moon Rooftop. So, if you ever visit Phnom Penh, be sure to stop by for a sundowner.

Air Conditioning

As for the hotel’s corridors, unlike most hotels, we do not provide air conditioning as people don’t stay long in those areas. The corridors are spacious enough to ensure good airflow.

However, rest assured, guests’ rooms are equipped with individual air conditioning systems.

After each guest’s departure or while cleaning the room, our room attendants will simply adjust the parameters back to 25º C and put it on low-level fan power.
