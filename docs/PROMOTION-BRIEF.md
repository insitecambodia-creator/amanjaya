# Promotion popup: what we need from the hotel

Please reply with the points below at least **5 working days before the start date**. We will send you a preview link to approve before it goes live.

1. **Name of the promotion** (for our records), e.g. "Pchum Ben 2026".
2. **First and last day** it should show (we count both days, Phnom Penh time).
3. **Headline** (about six words).
4. **Message** (one or two short sentences, about 160 characters). Please give the exact wording and any amounts or percentages you want shown.
5. **Small print / conditions** (optional), e.g. "Valid for stays until 31 December. Subject to availability."
6. **Photograph** (optional): a landscape photograph, at least 1600 px wide, that the hotel owns or may use, with a few words saying what it shows. Keep the subject in the middle.
7. **Button**: usually "Book Your Stay" (our booking page). If it should go somewhere else, send the exact link and the button wording.
8. **Where**: the homepage only (usual), or every page.
9. **Who approves** the preview on the hotel's side (name and email).

Notes:
- The popup appears once per visitor, a few seconds after the page opens, and stops by itself after the last day.
- It cannot be changed after it is live except by us: send corrections to the same address and we will update it.
